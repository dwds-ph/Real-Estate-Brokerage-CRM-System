## 1. Quick Start — First Login

### Step 1: Sign up / Log in

1. Open the CRM app in your browser.
2. If you received an **invitation** from your Broker, click the link in the
   email — it will take you straight to registration.
3. If you're registering directly, click **"Create account"** and fill in:
   - **Full name** (as it appears on your PRC / DHSUD license)
   - **Email address** (use the one you check daily)
   - **Mobile number** (important for SMS notifications)
   - **Password** (at least 8 characters)
4. After registration, check your email for the verification link and click it.

### Step 2: Set up your profile

1. On first login, you'll see the **Onboarding** screen.
2. Choose your **role**: Broker, Agent, or Sub-agent.
3. Fill in your professional details:
   - **License number** (PRC / DHSUD accreditation #)
   - **Accreditation body** (HLURB, DHSUD, PRC)
   - **Default commission rate** (your usual split)
4. Upload a **profile photo** — clients appreciate seeing your face.
5. Click **"Save & Continue"**.

### Step 3: Explore the dashboard

Once onboarded, you land on the **Dashboard**. Here's what you'll see:

- **KPI cards**: total leads, active listings, pending commissions
- **Today's schedule**: upcoming viewings and task deadlines
- **Quick actions**: "New Lead", "New Listing", "Schedule Viewing"
- **Recent activity**: latest updates from your team

> **💡 Tip:** Bookmark the CRM URL. Pin the tab — you'll be here a lot.

## 2. Understanding Roles: Broker vs Agent vs Sub-agent

The CRM has three roles, each with different permissions and visibility.

### 👑 Broker

- **Sees everything**: all agents, leads, listings, deals, commissions, expenses.
- **Manages agents**: invite new agents, set commission plans, assign leads.
- **Command center**: full analytics dashboard, agent leaderboard, P&L reports.
- **Approves**: payout requests, lead transfers, expense claims.

### 🕵️ Agent

- **Own data only**: sees own leads, listings, viewings, commissions, tasks.
- **Can have sub-agents**: broker may grant you sub-agents to help.
- **Submits requests**: payout requests, lead transfer requests, expense claims.
- **Uses all PH tools**: Pag-IBIG calculator, BIR estimator, title tracker.

### 🙋 Sub-agent / Assistant

- **Limited view**: only tasks and leads assigned to you.
- **No commission visibility**: you support the parent agent.
- **Can log activity**: update lead status, schedule viewings, upload docs.

## 3. Creating Your First Lead

### From the Dashboard

1. Click the **"New Lead"** button (top-right or on the quick-actions card).
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Client name** (required) — e.g. "Juan Dela Cruz"
   - **Contact number** (required) — mobile is best
   - **Email** — optional but nice to have
   - **Source** — where did this lead come from?
     - _Facebook_ (Marketplace inquiry, ad click)
     - _Referral_ (existing client, another agent)
     - _Walk-in_ (office visit, open house)
     - _Manual_ (other channels)
   - **Property interest** — type the project name, condo name, or area
   - **Budget range** — ₱ amount they're willing to spend
   - **Notes** — any relevant details (e.g. "Prefers low-floor unit, near LRT")
3. Click **"Save Lead"**.

### From the Leads Page

- Go to **Leads** in the sidebar → click **"+ Add Lead"**.
- Same form, but you stay on the Leads page after saving.

### Bulk Import (Brokers & Agents)

1. Go to **Leads** → click **"Import CSV"**.
2. Download the template (headers: name, phone, email, source, notes).
3. Fill in your leads, save as `.csv`, and upload.
4. The CRM will check for **duplicates** (same phone or email) and warn you.

> **✅ After saving:** Your new lead appears in the Kanban pipeline under
> **"New"** column. Time to move them forward!

## 4. Adding a Property Listing

### Step 1: Open the Listings page

Go to **Listings** in the sidebar → click **"+ Add Listing"**.

### Step 2: Fill in property details

The form has several sections:

#### 📋 Basic Info

- **Title** — e.g. "2BR Condo at SMDC Breeze Residences"
- **Description** — sell the property! Mention unique features.
- **Property Type** — Condo, House & Lot, Lot Only, Commercial, Foreclosed
- **Status** — Available (most common for new listings)

#### 💰 Pricing

- **Price** — the asking price in Philippine Pesos (₱)
- **Floor area** (sqm)
- **Lot area** (sqm, for houses)

#### 📍 Location

- **Address** — street, barangay
- **City** — e.g. Mandaluyong, Quezon City
- **Province** — e.g. Metro Manila, Cavite, Laguna
- **Flood risk** — Low, Medium, High, Unknown (very important in PH!)

#### 🏠 Property Details

- Bedrooms, bathrooms, floors, furnishing status
- **Amenities** — check all that apply: school, hospital, mall, LRT/MRT station

#### 📸 Media

- Upload **property photos** (drag & drop or click to select)
- You can add a **virtual tour URL** (e.g. Matterport, YouTube walkthrough)
- Upload **floor plan** (PDF or image)

### Step 3: Save & Publish

1. Click **"Save Listing"**.
2. The listing appears in your **Listings** page.
3. From here you can:
   - **Edit** details anytime
   - **Generate a brochure** (see Section 7)
   - **Mark as Sold / Under Option / Off-Market** when status changes

> **💡 Tip:** Good photos sell properties. Take bright, wide-angle shots on a
> sunny day. Avoid cluttered backgrounds.

## 5. Moving a Deal Through the Kanban Pipeline

The Kanban board is your **deal pipeline** — it shows every lead as a card
that moves through stages from "New" to "Closed".

### Accessing the Pipeline

- Go to **Deals** in the sidebar.
- You'll see columns: **New** → **Contacted** → **Viewed** → **Negotiating** → **Closed / Lost**

### Moving a deal

1. **Find the card** under its current column.
2. **Drag & drop** it to the next column.
   - _New_ → you've called them → _Contacted_
   - _Contacted_ → they've seen the property → _Viewed_
   - _Viewed_ → they're making an offer → _Negotiating_
   - _Negotiating_ → deal signed → _Closed_
   - Any stage → lead went cold → _Lost_

### Quick actions on a card

Click the **three dots (⋮)** on any card for:

- 📞 **Call** — opens your dialer with the client's number
- 💬 **Message** — opens Messenger / Viber / SMS
- 📅 **Schedule viewing** — jumps to the viewing scheduler
- 📝 **Add note** — quick note attached to this lead
- 👤 **View profile** — full lead details

### Broker view

If you're a **Broker**, the pipeline shows **all agents' deals grouped by
agent**. Click any agent's name to filter to just that agent.

> **✅ Best practice:** Update deal status immediately after every client
> interaction. This keeps your broker (and your commission pipeline) accurate.

## 6. Scheduling Viewings

### Quick schedule from anywhere

1. Click **"Schedule Viewing"** from:
   - The dashboard quick-actions card
   - A lead's detail page (button at top)
   - A deal card in the Kanban (⋮ menu)

### The viewing form

1. **Select lead** — pick from your assigned leads
2. **Select property** — pick from your listings
3. **Pick date & time** — use the date picker
4. **Duration** — default 1 hour, adjust as needed
5. **Add notes** — e.g. "Client wants to see the gym and pool too"

### After scheduling

- The viewing appears on your **Calendar** (see Section 10).
- The client receives a **confirmation link** you can send via Messenger/Viber.
- You'll get a **push notification 24 hours before** and **1 hour before**.

### During the viewing

1. Open the viewing in the **Viewings** page.
2. Tap **"Check In"** — take a photo at the property as proof.
3. After the viewing, fill in the **feedback form**:
   - Interest level: Low / Medium / High
   - Client concerns (e.g. "worried about flooding")
   - Next steps (e.g. "send payment computation")

### Post-viewing

- Update the lead status to **"Viewed"** on the Kanban.
- The feedback is saved to the lead's activity timeline.

## 7. Using PH-Specific Tools

### 🏦 Pag-IBIG Loan Calculator

Go to **PH Tools** → **Pag-IBIG Calculator**.

1. Enter the **property price** (₱)
2. Enter your **down payment %** (usually 10–20%)
3. Choose the **loan term** (5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30 years)
4. Enter the **interest rate** (check Pag-IBIG's current rate)
5. Click **"Compute"**

You'll see:

- **Loan amount** (property price minus down payment)
- **Monthly amortization** (principal + interest)
- **Max loanable amount** reference (Pag-IBIG tier table)
- **Total interest payable** over the full term

> **💡 Tip:** Share this with your buyer so they know their estimated monthly
> payment before they fall in love with a property.

### 🏛️ BIR Tax Estimator

Go to **PH Tools** → **BIR Tax Estimator**.

1. Enter the **deal / property price** (₱)
2. Click **"Compute"**

You'll see a breakdown of ALL taxes and fees:
| Tax / Fee | % | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Capital Gains Tax (CGT) | 6% | ₱XX,XXX |
| Documentary Stamp Tax (DST) | 1.5% | ₱XX,XXX |
| Creditable Withholding Tax (CWT) | 1% | ₱XX,XXX |
| Transfer Tax (provincial/city) | 0.5–0.75% | ₱XX,XXX |
| Registration Fee (RoD) | ~0.25% | ₱XX,XXX |
| **Total closing costs** | | **₱XX,XXX** |

> **💡 Tip:** Send this to buyers so there are no surprises at closing time.
> In PH real estate, closing costs are typically the buyer's responsibility.

### 📜 Title Status Tracker

Go to **Deals** → open a deal → scroll to **"Title Status"** section.

Track the title transfer through these stages:

1. **With Seller** — seller has the original title
2. **BIR (CGT)** — taxes being paid at BIR
3. **Registry of Deeds** — transfer being processed at RoD
4. **Transfer to Buyer** — new title in buyer's name
5. **Complete** — done!

For each stage you can:

- Mark as "Pending", "In Progress", or "Done"
- Upload required documents (e.g. BIR Form 1706, Tax Clearance, CAR)
- Add notes and dates
- See how long each stage historically took (timeline view)

### 📄 Property Brochure Generator

Go to **Listings** → click on a listing → **"Generate Brochure"**.

- Creates a **public, shareable page** with all property details
- Includes: main photo, details table, your contact info, QR code
- **Share via**: WhatsApp, Viber, Messenger, or copy link
- **Download as image** — perfect for posting on FB Marketplace
- **QR code** — print it on a flyer or business card

> **🔗 Example:** `yourapp.com/brochure/listing-abc123`

## 8. Commission Tracking

### How commissions work

Your Broker sets up **commission plans** that define how deals are split.

Common PH structures:

- **Fixed %** — e.g. 3% of selling price
- **Tiered split** — e.g. 50/50 broker/agent split
- **Referral fee** — fixed ₱ amount per referral
- **Co-broking** — two agents split one commission (listing agent + buyer's agent)

### View your commission on a deal

1. Open a **Deal** from the Kanban or Deals page.
2. Scroll to the **Commission** section.
3. You'll see:
   - **Deal price** (₱)
   - **Commission plan** name
   - **Gross commission** (deal price × commission rate)
   - **Your share** (after broker split)
   - **Tax deductions** (12% VAT, 1% Withholding Tax)
   - **Net commission**
   - If co-broking: both agents' shares displayed

### Request a payout

1. Go to **Commission** → **Payouts**.
2. Click **"Request Payout"** on a completed deal.
3. Your Broker receives a notification and can approve or reject.
4. Once approved, the payout status changes to "Paid" with a date.

### Commission forecast (Broker view)

Brokers can see:

- **Projected commissions** for the month (deals in Negotiating → Closing soon)
- **Earned vs collected** (which commissions are paid vs pending)
- **Per-agent report** (who's earning the most)

> **✅ Always verify:** The commission engine computes on the client side.
> Your deal data in Firestore is the source of truth. Double-check before
> submitting payouts.

## 9. Tasks and Reminders

### Creating a task

1. Go to **Tasks** in the sidebar → **"+ Add Task"**
2. Fill in:
   - **Title** — e.g. "Follow up with Juan re: BDO loan documents"
   - **Priority** — High / Medium / Low
   - **Due date**
   - **Link to** — optional: attach to a Lead, Listing, or Deal
   - **Description** — any details
3. Click **"Save"**

### Viewing tasks

- **Tasks page** — see all tasks, filter by priority or due date
- **Dashboard** — "My Tasks" widget shows tasks due today
- **Calendar** — tasks with due dates appear as orange events

### Marking tasks complete

- Check the box next to any task → it moves to "Done"
- Or open the task and click **"Mark Complete"**

### Broker assigning tasks

Brokers can assign tasks to agents:

1. Go to **Tasks** → **"+ Add Task"**
2. In the **"Assigned to"** field, pick an agent from your team
3. The agent receives a notification

### Smart reminders

The system automatically creates reminders for:

- **Inactive leads** — if a lead hasn't been contacted in 7 days → "Follow up
  with {lead}" task created
- **Viewing follow-up** — after a viewing, if no feedback in 24 hours →
  reminder to collect feedback
- **Document expiry** — documents expiring in 7 days → notification

## 11. Document Vault

PH real estate involves _a lot_ of documents. The Vault keeps them organised
per deal.

### Uploading a document

1. Go to **Vault** (sidebar) or open a **Deal** → **Documents** tab.
2. Click **"Upload Document"**.
3. Select the file(s) — supports PDF, images, Word, Excel.
4. Fill in:
   - **Document name** — e.g. "BIR Form 1706 - Signed"
   - **Category** — Title, Tax, Contract, ID, HOA, Miscellaneous
   - **Stage** — which title/mortgage stage this belongs to
   - **Expiry date** — if applicable (e.g. Tax Clearance expires)
5. Click **"Upload"**.

### Features

- **Version history** — upload a new version of the same doc → old version is
  kept. You can always go back.
- **Expiry alerts** — set an expiry date → you'll be notified 7 days before.
- **Document requests** — Broker can request a doc from Agent → notification
  with upload link.
- **Categories & tags** — easily filter and find any document.

### Common PH documents to upload

| Category | Documents                                    |
| -------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| Title    | Original TCT/CCT, Tax Declaration            |
| Tax      | BIR Form 1706, CAR, Tax Clearance, Receipts  |
| Contract | MOA/CPA, Deed of Sale, Reservation Agreement |
| ID       | Buyer's valid ID, Seller's valid ID          |
| HOA      | HOA Clearance, Association Dues              |

## 12. Expense Tracking

### Logging an expense

1. Go to **Expenses** in the sidebar → **"+ Add Expense"**
2. Fill in:
   - **Category** — Transportation, Meals, Ads, Miscellaneous
   - **Amount** (₱)
   - **Date**
   - **Note** — e.g. "Gas for client viewing in Tagaytay"
   - **Receipt photo** — snap and upload
   - **Link to deal** — optional, ties expense to a specific deal
3. Click **"Save"**

### Broker view

Brokers can optionally see all agent expenses. This helps with:

- Reimbursement processing
- P&L per agent (expenses vs commission earned)
- Tax deduction tracking (expenses are deductible for agents under BIR)

### Export

Go to **Expenses** → **"Export CSV"** — download your expenses for the year
for your own bookkeeping or BIR filing.

## 13. Best Practices

### 🏆 Daily habits for Agents

1. **Check your dashboard first thing** — see today's viewings and tasks.
2. **Update lead status immediately** — every call, viewing, or meeting should
   move the Kanban card.
3. **Log every communication** — even a 2-minute check-in call. It builds the
   activity timeline and helps your Broker.
4. **Upload documents right away** — don't wait. The Vault is only useful if
   it's current.
5. **Use the Pag-IBIG calculator proactively** — share it with buyers before
   they ask. It shows you're prepared.

### 👑 Best practices for Brokers

1. **Review the dashboard daily** — see which agents are active, which leads
   are stalling.
2. **Set up commission plans early** — agents work better when they know their
   split.
3. **Use the leaderboard** — healthy competition drives results.
4. **Approve payouts promptly** — happy agents = productive agents.
5. **Run the P&L report monthly** — know your office's financial health.

### 🔐 Security tips

- **Never share your password** — each agent has their own login.
- **Log out on shared devices** — especially at internet cafes or co-working
  spaces.
- **Be careful with client data** — leads contain personal info. Treat them
  like you'd want your own info treated.
- **Use strong passwords** — mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.

### ❓ Need help?

- Check the **in-app tooltips** (look for the ℹ️ icon)
- Ask your **Broker** (they have full access and can guide you)
- Check our documentation at **Help Center** (coming soon)
